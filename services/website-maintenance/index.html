Websites don't break all at once. They slowly fall apart.

A plugin update fails, a form stops sending, the site gets slower every month or something gets hacked. Usually you only find out when a customer tells you.

Our maintenance work catches these problems early. We keep everything updated, backed up and monitored, and fix things fast when something does go wrong.

WordPress, WooCommerce, Shopify, Magento, Squarespace, Wix and custom-coded websites.

Can you fix my hacked website?

Yes. We remove malware, find how it got in, patch the hole, harden security and help you get off Google's or your host's blocklist.

Do you need to have built my site?

No. We take over maintenance for sites built by other developers all the time. We start with a quick audit so we know what we're working with.

How do maintenance plans work?

You get a set amount of updates, monitoring, backups and development time each month. Need something bigger? We'll quote it separately.

How fast do you respond to problems?

Urgent issues like a down site or a broken checkout get priority. Most small fixes are done within one business day.
