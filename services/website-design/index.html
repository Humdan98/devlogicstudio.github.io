Questions people usually ask

Can't find your answer? Send us a quick email and you'll hear back within one business day.

hello@devlogicstudio.com
What do I get at the end of a design project?

You'll get organized Figma files with all pages and states, plus exported assets. If we're also building the site, we take it straight into development.

Can you design and build the website?

Yes, and that's where we're strongest. Having the same team design and develop means fewer surprises and a final site that matches the design.

How many revisions are included?

We agree on revision rounds at the start, but our goal is simple: we keep going until the design is right for your business.

Do you only design websites?

No. We also design logos, brand identities, mobile app screens, email templates and marketing graphics.

Can you redesign just part of my site?

Yes. We can redesign key pages like your homepage or product pages without touching the rest.
